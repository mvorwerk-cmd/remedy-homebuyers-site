# Remedy Home Buyers — Brand Guide

> All blog content, marketing copy, and customer communications must align with this guide.

## Tagline
**Sell Smart. Live Easy.**

## Mission
We help homeowners navigate tough situations — foreclosure, inheritance, divorce, code violations, relocation, distress — with honesty, fairness, and compassion. We are direct cash buyers, not agents. We close fast, take properties as-is, and pay all fees so sellers walk away with cash, no headaches, no obligations.

## Vision
A nationwide cash-buying business with a small-town soul: every homeowner who reaches out gets a fair offer, an honest conversation, and a path forward — even if that path doesn't end with selling to us.

## Core Values
1. **Relationships over transactions.** We're not chasing volume. Every seller is a person, often in a hard moment of life. We treat them that way.
2. **Honesty before profit.** We never take advantage of misfortune. We show our math, explain our offers, and tell people when selling to us is *not* their best option.
3. **Compassion in distress.** Sellers come to us because something hard is happening: a death in the family, a divorce, a missed mortgage payment, a property they can't afford to fix. We lead with empathy.
4. **Fair, transparent offers.** Our offers are based on the property's After-Repair Value (ARV) minus repair costs, our selling costs, and a fair profit. We walk every seller through the calculation.
5. **Win-win or no deal.** If the numbers don't work for both sides, we part ways as friends. No pressure tactics, no high-pressure closings.

## Voice & Tone
- **Plain English, not jargon.** Sellers we help often don't know terms like "ARV," "title encumbrance," or "judicial foreclosure." Define terms when we use them.
- **Warm but professional.** Like a knowledgeable friend who happens to buy houses, not a salesperson.
- **Educational, not promotional.** Blog posts should genuinely help readers — even ones who never sell to us. The goal is to be *the trusted resource* for distressed and unconventional home sales.
- **Confident, never pushy.** State facts. Explain options. Trust the reader to make their own decision.
- **Never alarmist.** Foreclosure is scary enough without sensational language. Calm, factual, action-oriented.

## What We Avoid
- "We'll buy your house no matter what!" (we're selective; this is dishonest)
- "Get an offer in 60 seconds!" (we give *fair* offers, not instant lowballs)
- Pressure phrases: "act now," "limited time," "you'll regret it if…"
- Fearmongering about foreclosure or financial distress
- Negative framing of real estate agents (they have their place; we offer a different option)
- Stock real-estate clichés ("dream home," "perfect property," "your forever home" — we don't sell, we buy)

## What We Emphasize
- **Speed when needed.** Close in as few as 7 days, or on the seller's timeline.
- **No fees, no commissions, no closing costs.** Sellers keep every dollar of our offer.
- **As-is sales.** No repairs, no cleaning, no inspections, no showings.
- **Certainty.** Cash offers don't fall through over financing. We close.
- **Honesty about trade-offs.** Cash offers are below retail. We're transparent that listing with an agent typically yields more on the top line — we win when speed, certainty, or as-is matter more than maximum price.

## Topics We Cover
- Foreclosure and pre-foreclosure (state-by-state laws, options, timelines)
- Inherited property and probate (state probate processes, multi-heir situations)
- Divorce and marital home division (state-by-state community-property vs. equitable-distribution rules)
- Code violations, condemned properties, fire damage
- Tenant-occupied sales
- Tax sales, liens, and back-taxes
- Out-of-state inheritance and relocation
- State and federal law updates affecting home sellers
- Local market overviews (median prices, days on market, who's buying)
- The cash-buyer process explained
- Honest comparisons: cash buyer vs. agent vs. iBuyer vs. FSBO
